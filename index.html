Staged Descent (Plan Descent, subfunction 2)
Minimum stage separation m

Default 300m (≈1000ft) - the minimum altitude gap a candidate intermediate stage must have from the cruise altitude, the intercept height, and any other chosen stage.

Max. worthwhile intermediate stages

Default 2 - stages are only worth stopping at if the wind there genuinely differs (direction/speed) from the surrounding levels; the biggest such shear points below cruise altitude are picked automatically, up to this many, rather than descending in many small fixed steps.

Quick Start

  • Landing Area (top-left toggle): always shows the currently predicted landing area, live.
  • Plan Descent has two subfunctions (small switch appears below it):
    • Quick Descent: tap anywhere on the map for a direct-descent plan to that point.
    • Staged Descent: drag the orange marker along the cyan reference trajectory to see every landing point reachable by pausing at intermediate altitudes, then tap inside that area for a specific stage-by-stage plan.
  • Header icons turn map layers on/off (power lines, roads/places, airspace, ground wind, nature reserves, non-landable terrain, experimental region names). A small dot shows loading (yellow) / ready (green).
  • Test Mode (top-right) lets you set a position manually - useful on the ground or if GPS drops out.
  • The red warning-triangle button (left of Test Mode) prepares an emergency position report for WhatsApp/SMS/Email.
  • Sliders under "Descent Parameters" drive both functions - drag the violet marker in Quick Descent, or use the Staged Descent sliders (total time budget, inter-stage rate) for the other subfunction.

⚠ Fill in Settings first - your aircraft registration, pilot contact numbers, and SMS/email recipients all need to be set up there before the emergency button can send a useful report.
